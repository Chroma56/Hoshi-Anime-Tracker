Copyright notices and takedown requests
Hoshi Anime respects intellectual property rights. Rights holders and authorised representatives can report specific material or links accessible through the project by opening an issue at Hoshi Anime GitHub Issues with the title Copyright Notice.

Please include:
A description of the copyrighted work you represent.
The Hoshi Anime page URL, relevant anime or manga title, and AniList ID if known.
The specific episode, chapter, stream link, or external source host concerned.
An explanation of the claimed infringement and your authority to submit the notice.
A means of arranging follow-up communication. Do not publish private contact details or sensitive documents in a public GitHub issue.
Notices will be reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible. Where appropriate and technically supported, the project may remove links, restrict affected titles, or disable access to particular third-party sources. If the material is hosted by another service, the rights holder may also need to contact that service to request removal from its servers.

Submitting a report does not mean content will automatically be blocked. Any actual blocking depends on the deployed website and backend functionality.
