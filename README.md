# Legal

**Hoshi Anime does not independently host, upload, store or distribute anime episodes, manga chapters or copyrighted streaming media.**

- Anime titles, artwork, descriptions and related information are obtained from AniList's public API and other third-party metadata providers.
- Anime episodes and manga chapters are made available by independent third-party websites and services, including external streaming and manga providers.
- Hoshi Anime provides an interface for discovering anime, watching episodes through supported players and reading manga from external sources.
- Some third-party services cannot be accessed directly by web browsers due to cross-origin restrictions. Hoshi Anime uses Cloudflare Workers and a separate backend to forward supported requests where necessary.
- Hoshi Anime supports embedded video players provided by third-party streaming services.
- When `HOSHI_RELAY_ENABLED=false` (recommended for public deployments), Hoshi's Node backend disables its signed media relay. Video playback may instead use supported external embedded players.
- When the media relay is enabled, supported video requests may pass through the backend during playback. Hoshi Anime does not intentionally maintain a permanent library of relayed video files.

## Copyright & Takedowns

Hoshi Anime respects intellectual property rights and takes copyright concerns seriously.

Copyright holders or their authorised representatives may submit a takedown request regarding content or external sources accessible through Hoshi Anime.

To submit a copyright notice:

1. Visit [Hoshi Anime GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Chroma56/Hoshi-Anime-Tracker/issues).
2. Create an issue titled **"Copyright Notice"**.
3. Identify the copyrighted work concerned.
4. Provide the relevant anime or manga title, AniList ID (if available), and URL.
5. Identify the allegedly infringing content or third-party source.
6. Provide sufficient information to establish your authority to act on behalf of the rights holder and a means of contact.

Valid notices will be reviewed as promptly as reasonably possible.

Where appropriate, Hoshi Anime may:

- Remove or restrict access to identified anime episodes or manga chapters.
- Disable links to specific external streaming or manga sources.
- Block affected titles or providers from supported backend services.
- Take additional steps required by applicable copyright law.

Hoshi Anime cannot remove content hosted by independent third-party providers. Copyright holders should also contact the relevant hosting service directly.
