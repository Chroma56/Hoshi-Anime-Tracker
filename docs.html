Hoshi

An anime and manga tracker with its own player and reader. These pages explain how it works, where your data is kept, and the rules that come with using it.

Getting started

Install on Windows

Hoshi for Windows is the same Hoshi as the website, in a window of its own. It is built with Rust and uses the web engine Windows already has, so the installer is about one megabyte.

  1. Open the latest release.
  2. Download Hoshi-Setup to install it, or Hoshi-Portable to run it without installing.
  3. Windows will say the publisher is unknown, because the app is not code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

What the app adds

  • Your library and progress are kept by the app, so no browser setting can clear them.
  • Pop-up windows from embedded players are refused.
  • It updates itself: the app shows the live site, so new features arrive the next time you open it.

The app keeps its own library, separate from your browser's. To bring yours across, see Profiles and backups.

Getting started

Install on a phone

Hoshi installs from the browser, with no app store.

  • Android (Chrome, Brave, Edge): open Hoshi, open the browser menu, and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): open Hoshi, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

You get the star icon and a full-screen app. On a desktop browser the same thing is offered on the Get the app page.

Getting started

Finding and saving shows

The pill menu at the bottom of the screen moves between the main pages.

  • Home: the banner, what is trending, this season, and Continue watching.
  • Manga: manga, manhwa and manhua, with their own search.
  • Discover: filter by genre, format, season, year and status.
  • Search: finds anime and manga by title.
  • Schedule: what airs each day, in your local time.
  • Library: everything you saved.

Open a title and press Save to add it to your library. Playing an episode or opening a chapter adds it to Continue watching or Continue reading by itself. The Hoshi logo at the top right always returns to the home page.

Getting started

Where your data lives

Hoshi has no accounts. Your library, watch progress, reading progress, profile and settings are stored on your own device, in the browser's storage or in the desktop app's.

  • Nothing is uploaded, so nobody else can see it and it does not follow you to another device by itself.
  • A browser can erase it: clearing site data, private windows, and some privacy settings all do. If the browser refuses to save at all, Hoshi shows a notice at the top of the page.
  • The desktop app's storage is not affected by browser settings.

To protect against loss, or to move to another device, use a profile file.

Getting started

Profiles and backups

The round button at the top right opens Profiles. A profile is a name, a picture, a short line about you and up to twelve favourites.

Share as file

Downloads a file ending in .hoshi.json that holds your profile, your library and your progress. It doubles as a backup.

Import a profile

Pick a profile file and say whose it is:

  • A friend's: it appears under "From friends", with their favourites and library to browse.
  • Mine: it restores your profile, library and progress onto this device. It only adds; where a show is in both places, the more recent progress wins.

A profile file contains your whole library and progress. Anyone you send it to can see those lists.

Watching

The player

When you play an episode, Hoshi looks for a stream it can play in its own player. While it looks, the source's embedded player starts, so there is never a wait. When the stream is found, Hoshi's player takes over at the same episode.

  • It resumes. Your position is saved every few seconds and when you pause or leave.
  • It fills the page. The episode list sits underneath, and F or a double click goes full screen.
  • Settings live inside the picture. The CC button opens subtitles, speed, quality and subtitle styling.
  • It recovers. A stalled stream is retried, and a frozen picture is rebuilt, before it gives up.
  • On touch screens: tap to show or hide the controls, double tap either side to skip ten seconds, and hold to play at double speed.

The label in the bar under the video says which player is running: Direct stream for Hoshi's, Embed for the source's.

Watching

Subtitles

Hoshi's player lists every subtitle language the stream offers and loads the one you prefer. Change the preferred language in Settings, or switch on the spot with the CC button or C.

In the player's settings you can change the text size, colour, background and position, shift the timing with an offset if the subtitles run early or late, and load your own .vtt or .srt file.

Watching

Skip intro and up next

When the source marks where the opening is, a Skip intro button appears during it (S does the same). Turn on Skip intros automatically in Settings to skip without pressing anything.

When the credits start, or the episode ends, an Up next card counts down to the next episode. Cancel stops it for that episode; turn off Autoplay next episode in Settings to stop it for good.

Not every episode has these marks. Where the source has none, the buttons do not appear.

Watching

Embed fallback

The embedded player is the source's own. Hoshi uses it when it cannot find a stream for its own player, or when that stream fails. The embed has its own controls, and Hoshi's shortcuts, subtitle styling and skip button do not apply to it.

  • Embed · Still looking… Hoshi is asking again; it tries three times.
  • Embed · No direct stream No stream was found. Try direct player in the bar asks once more.
  • Embed · Direct failed A stream was found but would not play.

The first episode after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to switch, because the stream server sleeps when nobody is using it. To always start in the embed, set Player to Embed in Settings.

Reading

The manga reader

Open a manga, manhwa or manhua and pick a chapter. The reader opens over the page.

  • Scroll reads top to bottom, which suits webtoons. Single page shows one page at a time.
  • Fit sizes a page to the width, to the screen, or shows it at its original size, with zoom on top.
  • Source chooses where chapters come from. Auto checks each source and reads from the one that is furthest along.
  • Language picks which translation to list.

Your place in each series is saved and shows up under Continue reading on the home page. Chapter availability depends on the source; if one has nothing, try another.

Copyright and takedowns

Hoshi respects the rights of creators and rights holders.

What Hoshi does and does not hold

Hoshi does not upload, store or keep copies of any video, chapter or page. Everything shown comes from third-party sites at the moment it is requested. When Hoshi's own player is used, a relay server passes the stream along from the third-party host as it is watched; it does not keep it.

Reporting content

If you are a rights holder, or act for one, and believe something reachable through Hoshi infringes your rights, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with:

  • the work you own or represent;
  • the title, and the address of the Hoshi page where it appears;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised, and that your notice is accurate;
  • your name and a way to reach you.

Please do not put private contact details in a public issue; say that you would like to be contacted and a private channel will be arranged.

What happens next

Valid notices are reviewed and the title is removed from Hoshi's listings or blocked from its player. Because the content itself is hosted by third parties, removing it from the internet requires contacting that host directly.

Counter-notice

If something was removed by mistake, reply on the same issue explaining why, and it will be reviewed again.

Policies

Privacy Policy

Hoshi is built to know as little about you as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising or analytics code in Hoshi itself.

What stays on your device

Your library, watch and reading progress, profile (name, picture, favourites), friends' profiles you imported, and settings are stored in your browser's or the app's local storage. They are not sent to Hoshi. Clearing site data, or the buttons in Settings, removes them.

What Hoshi's servers see

To find and play a stream, your device asks Hoshi's servers for an episode, and the relay fetches the video on your behalf. Like any web server, they see your IP address and what was requested while handling it.

  • Hoshi keeps no database of users or of what anyone watches.
  • Short-lived technical logs (for example which host a stream came from, and errors) are kept by the hosting providers for troubleshooting.
